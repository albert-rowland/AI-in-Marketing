# 04 · Prepare social content

[← Course home](../README.md) · [Prompt index](../prompts/README.md)

Prepare six post packages and a dated calendar proposal that you can review before any calendar write.

All input paths below start inside your extracted project folder. Provide the named files in Work, or open that folder as your local Codex project.

## Prompt 09 · Create content agent

| Before you paste | Your action |
| --- | --- |

| **Provide these inputs** | agents/content_creator.md and .codex/agents/content_creator.toml |
| **Expect this output** | A verified Content Creator role with six post folders and a draft schedule. |

Copy the complete text below into the message field and submit it once. Wait for the app to finish before inspecting the returned files.

[Open the copyable text file](../prompts/09-create-content-agent.txt)

```text
Read agents/content_creator.md and the corresponding .codex/agents/content_creator.toml. Verify the local Codex definition or use the Markdown role as specialist instructions in Work. Confirm the output structure includes six post folders, copy, visual briefs, prompts, alt text, and a draft schedule.
```

**Review before continuing.** Confirm copy, visual briefs, prompts, alt text, and schedule outputs.

**Next step.** Continue to prompt 10 and request social content.

## Prompt 10 · Slack social handoff

| Before you paste | Your action |
| --- | --- |
| **Open this app** | A permitted classroom Slack DM or channel connected to Onigiri |
| **Provide these inputs** | Project files, approved Discovery Box brief, Growth Strategist and Content Creator role files |
| **Expect this output** | Six post folders, a review PDF, and a relative-day schedule CSV. |

Copy the complete text below into the message field and submit it once. Wait for the app to finish before inspecting the returned files.

[Open the copyable text file](../prompts/10-slack-social-handoff.txt)

```text
Onigiri, use the attached Copper Cup project and the approved Discovery Box brief to prepare two weeks of social content. Delegate audience and campaign checks to the Growth Strategist and post creation to the Content Creator using their supplied role files. Wait for both results, reconcile the offer and CTA, and return six post folders, a review PDF, and a schedule CSV. Keep the schedule in relative days until I provide a start date and time zone. Bring the package to me for review before any calendar write or publication.
```

**Review before continuing.** Wait for both specialists, check the reconciled offer and CTA, and review every post.

**Next step.** Continue to prompt 11 with your chosen start date and time zone.

## Prompt 11 · Calendar review

| Before you paste | Your action |
| --- | --- |
| **Open this app** | ChatGPT Work or Onigiri with permitted calendar access |
| **Provide these inputs** | Reviewed post folders, schedule.csv, your start date, and your time zone |
| **Expect this output** | A dated calendar proposal displaying every entry, destination, and connected account. |

Copy the complete text below into the message field and submit it once. Wait for the app to finish before inspecting the returned files.

[Open the copyable text file](../prompts/11-calendar-review.txt)

```text
Use the six reviewed post folders and schedule.csv. Prepare a dated calendar plan for the start date and time zone that I provide. If either input is missing, ask for it. Show every proposed entry, its content, the destination calendar, and the connected account. Wait for my specific approval before writing to Notion or another connected calendar. Read back approved entries after writing.
```

**Review before continuing.** Approve a specific calendar write only after reviewing all entries. Read back any approved writes.

**Next step.** Continue to Lesson 5 and review project routing.
