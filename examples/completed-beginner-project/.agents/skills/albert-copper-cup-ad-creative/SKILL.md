# Copper Cup Ad Creative

## Process
Read AGENTS.md, brand/context.md, and the Current campaign section. Identify the requested audience need and preserve its hypothesis label. Draft three concepts unless the assignment requests one. Include a headline, body, CTA, audience rationale, and source for every product claim. Read the chosen concept before proposing visual direction, an image prompt, and planned alt text.

## Review
Check the three 100 g bags, USD 29, USD 5 shipping, and supported CTA. Keep sourcing, certification, health, sustainability, testimonial, discount, and performance claims pending evidence. Inspect visual accuracy before describing a finished image. Label alt text planned until that review.

## Completion
Save the requested files in the named campaign directory. Return paths, source checks, gaps, and the next owner. Text drafts and image prompts complete the classroom task. Image generation and external actions require their own tool access and human decision.
