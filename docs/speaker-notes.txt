SLIDE 01 · Build Your AI Marketing Team with Agents

TEACHING SCRIPT
This single 60-minute class begins with an interview that creates the brand context and project instructions. We then create a skill, define six roles, and start specialist assignments. A coordinated campaign demonstrates independent handoffs and review. We will also see Dot and voice, a private campaign library, and a recurring-brief proposal before your ten-minute exercise. Copper Cup and its campaign figures are fictional teaching examples.

TEACHING TIME
Allow 1 minute within this single 60-minute session.
