## Primary video
Grace Leung, "ChatGPT Work + Dot: Build your AI Marketing Team (Agents + Skills)", October 3, 2026, 15 minutes 58 seconds.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BzS93V2zFTg

The adaptation preserves this teaching order. 00:27 explains Work, Codex, and Dot. 01:20 sets up Dot. 02:03 introduces the lead and specialists. 02:34 establishes brand context. 03:27 creates the ad skill. 06:02 creates the website skill and Dot handoff. 07:49 distinguishes skills and agents. 08:27 creates the Growth Strategist. 09:51 creates social content through Slack. 12:34 updates routing and coordinates a lead campaign. The sponsor segment becomes a short context explanation, with promotional claims omitted.

The deck clarifies six total roles, one lead and five specialists. The coffee brand replaces Lexora. Original coffee artwork and native diagrams replace source visuals. The local form demonstrates validation and PDF delivery without implying a production CRM integration.
