# Discovery Box Campaign Brief

Fictional campaign for classroom practice. Status remains draft for review.

## Goal and audience
Prepare a Discovery Box launch for busy professionals who brew coffee at home. The exercise goal is to produce a coherent review package, with offer accuracy and a clear CTA as the acceptance criteria.

## Offer and channels
Three 100 g coffee bags for USD 29 plus USD 5 shipping. Create three Instagram ad concepts, a one-page offer site, and six social posts across two weeks. Use Explore the Discovery Box throughout those assets.

## Deliverables
Save ad concepts and prompts, image assets, an offer-page preview, strategy.md, six post folders, a review PDF, and a schedule CSV. Mark each asset as a fictional demo. Use relative paths inside the project.

## Review criteria
Every product claim comes from brand/context.md. The price and shipping stay consistent. The main CTA is visible. Images use the approved palette. Each post has alt text. The page adapts to mobile. Approval covers the draft content separately from any future external action.
