# Brewing Guide Campaign Brief

Fictional campaign for classroom practice. Status remains draft for review.

## Goal
Create a ten-page home-brewing guide and a local download-page demo. Use data/past-campaigns.csv to explain the test hypothesis. Review lead conversion and cost per lead separately from Discovery Box purchases.

## Audience and deliverables
Help busy home brewers choose a repeatable brewing routine. Create a guide with clear starting ratios, a troubleshooting worksheet, and a restrained Discovery Box CTA. The landing page should display the guide cover and offer its PDF through a working demo form. Provide a measurement plan and a review checklist.

## Classroom form boundary
Use a fictional test address such as learner@example.com. Validate its format in the browser and provide the PDF link. Keep the submitted address in the current page session only. Production lead storage, privacy language, opt-in handling, email delivery, spam protection, and deployment require separate integration and review.
