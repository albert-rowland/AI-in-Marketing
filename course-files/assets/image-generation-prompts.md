# Image Generation Prompts

These three assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and inspected for this fictional workshop. They represent fictional products and scenes. The artwork is reused across a coherent campaign, with matching product and guide images on the pages.

## Product artwork
Create an editorial product photograph for fictional Copper Cup Coffee. Show three matte cream bags with deep teal labels bearing COPPER CUP and DISCOVERY BOX, a copper measuring scoop, and a white ceramic mug on a warm stone kitchen counter. Use soft morning window light and the approved palette. Compose a wide image with products on the right and open space on the left. Exclude certifications, badges, prices, and unrelated writing.

## Brewing artwork
Create an editorial photograph of a hand pouring from a copper gooseneck kettle into a ceramic coffee dripper above a cream mug. Use a teal towel and warm cream counter. Compose a wide image with the dripper on the right and open space on the left. Exclude text and logos.

## Desk artwork
Create an editorial photograph of a cream mug beside a closed teal notebook and a cream bag marked COPPER CUP on a home office desk. Use morning light, espresso wood, and a copper pencil. Compose a wide image with the mug on the right. Exclude additional writing, screens, and badges.
