# Copper Cup Coffee

This is a fictional training brand. Every product detail, price, audience assumption, and performance figure belongs to this exercise.

## Product and offer
The Discovery Box contains three 100 g bags, one light roast, one medium roast, and one dark roast. The training price is USD 29 plus USD 5 shipping. Use those figures only inside this fictional campaign. The brand sells the box as a first purchase, with a home-brewing guide available as a separate download.

## Audience
Busy professionals who brew coffee at home and want help choosing a roast. They value a repeatable morning routine and clear brewing instructions. Treat that audience description as a hypothesis to test.

## Voice and claims
Use warm, specific language with restrained enthusiasm. Explain the roast choices and give clear next steps. Use the approved product details above. Leave sourcing certifications, sustainability claims, health benefits, awards, customer quotes, and sales outcomes out of campaign copy until a source supports them.

## Visual direction
Cream #FAF4EA, espresso #2B2118, teal #087F8C, copper #C76A42. Use Arial or a comparable sans serif with generous spacing. Pair large coffee photography with short headlines. Preserve readable contrast across every asset. Refer to assets/ for generated fictional artwork and the brand wordmark.

## Primary CTAs
Offer page and ads use Explore the Discovery Box. The lead campaign uses Download the brewing guide. Classroom pages demonstrate the path without taking payment or collecting personal data.
