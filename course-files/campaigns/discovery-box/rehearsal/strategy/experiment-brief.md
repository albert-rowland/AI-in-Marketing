# Experiment Brief

## Hypothesis
Roast-discovery and morning-routine angles may produce different Discovery Box purchase rates.

## Design
Compare two concepts with a shared offer and page. Confirm audience, allocation method, spend cap, run duration, attribution window, and stopping rule before launch. Use purchases and cost per order as the primary outcomes for this offer campaign. Report leads in a separate measure.

## Decision
Return the observed metrics with sample sizes and limitations. The reviewer chooses the next test and spending decision after reviewing the evidence.
