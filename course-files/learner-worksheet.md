# Ten-Minute Learner Exercise

## Goal
Adapt one Discovery Box ad for people setting up their first home office. Preserve the product, price, shipping, and palette.

## Minute 0 to 2
Read the supplied brand context and campaign brief files. Write one sentence describing the revised audience need.

## Minute 2 to 6
Run prompts/14-guided-exercise.txt with the ad skill. Use supplied artwork if image generation takes too long. Record the headline, body copy, CTA, visual brief, and alt text.

## Minute 6 to 9
Check the offer, claims, CTA, and palette. Identify one revision with evidence from the brief.

## Minute 9 to 10
Share the revised concept and explain the audience change. Keep publication as a later decision.

## Review rubric
Give one point each for supported product details, audience-specific wording, consistent CTA, clear visual direction, and useful alt text. A five-point result passes the classroom review. This rubric assesses the draft rather than commercial performance.
