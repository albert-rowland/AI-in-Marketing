# Copper Cup Campaign Strategy

## Process
Read the goal, brand context, approved brief, and supplied data. Separate sourced facts, fictional assumptions, and recommendations. Choose a primary outcome with a defined denominator. Propose a bounded test and explain the tradeoff between lead volume and purchases. Save strategy.md and experiment-brief.md with the measurement plan and required approvals. Completion requires a traceable recommendation and a clear review decision.

## Project boundary
Use fictional training material from the project root. Follow the project approval rules. Keep uncertain claims labeled and preserve editable sources.
