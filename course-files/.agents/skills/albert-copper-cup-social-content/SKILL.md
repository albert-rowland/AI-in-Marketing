# Copper Cup Social Content

## Process
Read the approved brief and brand context. Produce the requested quantity and duration. For six posts across two weeks, create six folders containing copy.md, visual-brief.md, image-prompt.md, and alt-text.md. Use distinct audience angles with consistent offer details. Prepare a review summary and a draft schedule CSV. Stop at a review package until the user approves the specific scheduling or publication action.

## Project boundary
Use fictional training material from the project root. Follow the project approval rules. Keep uncertain claims labeled and preserve editable sources.
