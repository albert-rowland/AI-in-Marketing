# Copper Cup Lead Magnet

## Process
Read the approved lead brief and brand context. Outline ten pages with a clear audience benefit. Include a ratio starting point, a troubleshooting worksheet, and an action page. Qualify brewing suggestions as starting points that vary with equipment and taste. Create editable source and a PDF, then render and inspect every page. Reuse its cover on the landing page. Save the source, PDF, cover, and review checklist.

## Project boundary
Use fictional training material from the project root. Follow the project approval rules. Keep uncertain claims labeled and preserve editable sources.
