## Insert each recording

Select the label shapes inside the video frame and remove those labels. Insert your Google Drive video and size it to x 256 pt, y 110 pt, width 678 pt, and height 381.375 pt. Keep the prompt panel at 226 pt wide. In the PDF export, the frame remains a recording placeholder.

## Before recording

Use fictional Copper Cup sources and a prepared output for each demonstration. Hide unrelated chats and account details. Cut generation waiting time, narrate the source check, and hold the reviewed result for five seconds. Preview each recording during a classroom rehearsal.
