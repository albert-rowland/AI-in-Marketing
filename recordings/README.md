# Record your course demonstrations

[← Return to the course](../README.md)

The demonstration slides reserve one quarter for the prompt and three quarters for a recording. Their speaker notes include the complete copyable prompt and a recording checklist. Keep the recording focused on the visible task, its output, and one review check.

Open [the complete instructor notes](../docs/instructor-notes.md) for the spoken script and full prompts. The clip plays within the listed teaching time. Explain the steps during playback, then pause for questions.

## Record each demonstration

1. Open the correct course project and confirm the required files are available.
2. Open the app named in the lesson guide and prepare a clean recording view.
3. Copy the full prompt from the speaker notes or linked lesson guide.
4. Record the prompt submission, the relevant response, and the saved output.
5. Cut extended waiting periods and keep the sequence understandable.
6. Show the review check and explain the next step before ending.
7. Place the recording inside the slide's large marked video area.
8. Rehearse playback with the speaker notes visible on your presenter screen.

Record at 1920 by 1080. Hide credentials, personal messages, account details, and unrelated tabs. Leave the reviewed output visible for five seconds.

## Insert each recording

For Google Slides, upload a recording to your own Drive and use Insert > Video > Google Drive. Remove the video frame's label shapes and fit the recording to the marked 16:9 frame. Confirm the intended learners have video access before sharing a deck containing that recording. The PDF preserves a static slide frame, so use the editable deck for video playback.

This course ships with labeled recording placeholders. Add your captured recordings before teaching with video playback.
